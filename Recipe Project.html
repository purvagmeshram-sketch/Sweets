Gulab Jamun

Ingredients

  • Sugar
  • Khoya
  • Oil

Recipe

  1. Boil Syrup: Boil sugar and water until slightly sticky, add cardamom or rose water, and keep warm.
  2. Mix Dough: Combine mawa, flour, and a pinch of baking soda, then gently knead into a soft dough using a little milk.
  3. Shape and Fry: Roll the dough into smooth, crack-free balls and fry them in low-heat ghee or oil until deep golden brown.
  4. Soak: Drop the warm jamuns directly into the warm syrup and let them soak for two to four hours until juicy.

Barfi

Ingredients

  • Sugar
  • Rava
  • Ghee

Recipe

  1. Heat one-fourth cup of ghee in a pan, add one cup of rava (semolina), and roast it on low heat for about 7 to 8 minutes until it becomes fragrant without changing its color.
  2. In a separate saucepan, combine three-fourth cup of sugar and half a cup of water, then boil the mixture until it reaches a one-string consistency.
  3. Lower the heat, pour the warm sugar syrup into the roasted rava, add half a teaspoon of cardamom powder, and stir continuously until the mixture thickens and starts leaving the sides of the pan.
  4. Transfer the mixture immediately into a greased tray, smooth the top surface with a spatula, decorate with chopped nuts, and slice into diamond or square shapes once it cools down completely.

Croissant

Ingredients

  • Puff pastry
  • Chocolate
  • Eggs

Recipe

  1. Mix a soft yeast dough and chill it thoroughly in the refrigerator.
  2. Roll out the dough, wrap it around a flat block of cold butter, and repeatedly fold and roll it to create hundreds of thin layers.
  3. Roll the laminated dough thin, cut it into triangles, and roll them up tightly from the wide end to the tip.
  4. Let the croissants rise in a warm spot until doubled in size, then bake them in a hot oven until golden brown and flaky.

Pastry

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Butter
  • Cold Milk

Recipe

  1. Sift flour into a bowl, add cold cubed butter, and rub them together using your fingertips until the mixture looks like fine breadcrumbs.
  2. Gradually add a little cold water (or milk) to the crumb mixture.
  3. Gently bring the mixture together with your hands on a clean surface to form a soft dough, being careful not to overwork it.
  4. : Wrap the dough in plastic wrap and chill it in the refrigerator for at least 20 to 30 minutes before rolling out.

Ghevar

Ingredients

  • Maida
  • Ghee
  • Chilled Water

Recipe

  1. Prepare the batter by whisking ghee with ice until creamy, then blending in flour and ice-cold water to create a thin, smooth consistency.
  2. Fry the disc by pouring the batter in a steady stream into a deep pot of smoking hot ghee, allowing it to foam and form a webbed structure.
  3. Drain excess grease by lifting the golden-brown ghevar out of the pot using a skewer and placing it onto a wire rack.
  4. Sweeten and garnish by ladling warm sugar syrup over the cooled disc and topping it with thickened rabri and chopped nuts.
